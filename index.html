Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers
Which photos should I send?

Choose clear original photos with visible eyes and natural light. Send several favourites and I will help you choose. Photo Tips ⟶

How long will my portrait take?

We agree a schedule before booking. Painting, drying and delivery each take time, so please mention any special date in your enquiry.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Carefully packed, insured worldwide shipping is included. Any applicable import charges remain the recipient’s responsibility.
