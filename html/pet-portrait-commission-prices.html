Ask about a size ⟶

Which size suits your portrait?

30 × 25, 40 × 30 and 45 × 35 cm are for one pet. For two or more pets, we start with 50 × 40 cm or larger and choose a format to suit the composition.

A close-up portrait and a full-body painting need different amounts of space. Send your favourite photographs and tell me where the painting will hang, and I will help you choose. The listed sizes are examples; other formats are available on request. Inch measurements are approximate.

Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers
Can I include more than one pet?

Yes. We plan a format that gives each animal enough space. The three smallest sizes are for one pet; for multiple pets, we start with 50 × 40 cm or larger. Multiple pets and the agreed background are included in the price for your chosen canvas size.

Can you work from older photographs?

Send the best images you have and I will assess what is possible before you book.

What happens after I approve my painting?

You pay the remaining 50%. Once the painting is ready to travel, it is carefully packed and dispatched with insured shipping.
