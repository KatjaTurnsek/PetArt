I’m Katja Turnsek. I create original oil portraits for people who share their lives with animals.

A connection to animals.
A lifelong love of painting.

Meet Katja

The personality behind the portrait.

Animals have been part of my imagination for as long as I can remember. As a child, while friends dreamed of horses, I longed for a dog of my own. Painting became a way to explore that fascination.

Today, I work with clients around the world from my studio in Slovenia. My Border Collie, Roi, is a familiar presence beside the easel.

Over the years, I have also worked in pastel, watercolour and pencil. Today, my portrait commissions focus on oils on linen, building up layers and details to bring an animal’s expression to life.

My approach

A likeness, with life in it.

A tilt of the head. Light caught in the eyes. The softness of a familiar expression. I look for the details that make an animal recognisable to the people who know them best.

I work from your photographs and help you choose the images that best show your animal’s appearance and character. Together, we plan the size, background and composition. You approve a digital mock-up before I begin painting.

The portrait develops in layers, with close attention to the eyes, fur and the relationship between light and shadow. I share photographs by email as I work, so you can follow the painting from the planned composition to the finished portrait.
